# Revealer

Scripture memory match. Flip two cards. Pair the verse with its reference.

Play: https://pastoremma19.github.io/revealer/

## Levels

1. Little Lights — 3 pairs, a long peek, 3 hints
2. Pathfinders — 4 pairs, a short peek, 2 hints
3. Keepers of the Word — 6 pairs, no peek, 2 hints
4. Watchmen — 8 pairs, no peek, 1 hint
5. Scribes — 10 pairs, no peek, 1 hint

Best time and moves are saved in the browser per level.
